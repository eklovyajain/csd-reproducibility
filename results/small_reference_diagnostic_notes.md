# Small-Reference Boundary Diagnostic

Data: deidentified N = 451, all-mpnet-base-v2 (existing cache, no new embeddings), canonical
`csd_decompose`. All 451 statuses `ok`. reference_magnitude = ||C − I||.
Generated by `small_reference_diagnostic.py`. Supporting files:
`results/reference_magnitude_summary.csv`, `results/small_reference_diagnostic.csv`,
`results/reference_magnitude_orientation.csv`. The qualitative flags are one reader's judgments
of the 20 smallest-reference cases, not computed quantities; they do not define a cutoff.

**A. Are there observations where C is so close to I that the reference axis appears substantively meaningless?**
No. The minimum ||C − I|| is 0.602, with max cos_IC = 0.819. The reference distribution is
narrow, with min 0.602 and p1 0.669. Its low end sits well above the low end of revision
magnitude, whose minimum is 0.093. No case among the 20 smallest was judged `no`. Two are
`caution`:
- Row 26: the critique largely reuses risks the initial already named.
- Row 101: the initial text summarizes the scenario without stating a decision, so the critique
  answers a recommendation that was never made.

**B. Do the 20 smallest-reference cases contain genuinely distinct critiques?**
Yes: 17 `clearly_distinct` and 3 `small_but_real` (rows 26, 199, 256), with none `minimal`.
Every critique challenges a specific premise of the initial reasoning and adds at least one
consideration the initial did not raise. In the smallest-reference cases, the closeness of C to
I appears to come from shared case vocabulary and from critiques that restate the initial claim
before challenging it. It does not appear to come from a missing argument.

**C. Does normalized orientation look pathological among very small-reference cases?**
No.
- **Bottom 5% of reference magnitude** (n = 23, ||C − I|| ≤ 0.737): aligned_normalized mean
  0.335 (SD 0.133), range 0.049–0.605.
- **Remaining 95%** (n = 428): mean 0.420 (SD 0.145), range 0.027–0.876.
- **Spread:** the small-reference values lie within the bulk of the distribution, and none is
  extreme.
- **Correlations, full sample:**
  - aligned_normalized: r = .28, ρ = .25.
  - residual_normalized: r = −.25, ρ = −.25.

These are weaker than the revision-magnitude correlations (about .49). Reference magnitude is
itself correlated with revision magnitude (r = .26), so the two associations overlap and
shouldn't be read as independent. The figures are descriptive only, with no test.

**D. Is there evidence that a minimum ||C − I|| threshold is needed?**
No. In this sample, no reference is near zero, the smallest cases contain distinct critiques,
and orientation in the low tail is not extreme. A threshold drawn from these data would remove
interpretable cases.
